# Carol Build Reference

Purpose: supporting rules, grant sources and crafting options for [Carol's accepted tabletop sheet](../Character%20Sheets/Carol%20Elfnein.md). Status: documentation only, updated 2026-10-18; production JSON has not been migrated to this sheet. The sheet owns current statistics, inventory, spell selections and book counts; this reference owns the explanations below.

Decisions: Human/Sage and the Wizard/Alchemist build are selected. Nature, Medicine, Insight and the starting spell package are settled. The [spellbook policy](../Systems/Spell%20Containers%20and%20Consumable%20Sources.md#books-and-spell-readiness), [container rules](../Systems/Spell%20Containers%20and%20Consumable%20Sources.md) and [Aetherlock rules](Aetherlock%20Engineered%20Rounds.md) own their respective campaign exceptions and recipes.

Questions: minor equipment handling, body size if an explicit selection is needed, acquisition history and the Guise component's appearance remain unspecified. None blocks the current [systems design](../Systems/Campaigns%20and%20Shared%20Data.md).

## Build provenance

Human's Resourceful supplies daily Heroic Inspiration, Skillful supplies Medicine, and Versatile supplies Alert, including its willing-ally initiative exchange. Sage supplies Arcana, History, Calligrapher's Supplies and Magic Initiate (Wizard, INT). Wizard supplies Investigation and Insight, Scholar supplies Arcana Expertise, and Skill Expert supplies Perception and Investigation Expertise. The selected revised-Artificer multiclass skill is Nature: natural-world knowledge supports the alchemy/herbalism concept without duplicating existing grants.

The revised multiclass transcription also supplies Tinker's Tools proficiency and armor training; Alchemist supplies Alchemist's Supplies and Herbalism proficiency. These class grants are separate from equipment ownership and Manifold's conditional proficiency.

The sheet's Sage reconstruction costs 27 points (0+9+5+9+2+2), then applies background, class/feat and DM-earned Indomitable increases. It reconstructs accepted values rather than claiming a historical level-up ledger. Indomitable is earned during play: +2 to one chosen ability (INT for Carol), with no creation-point cost and no freely repeatable or reassignable benefit. Fixed-average HP is calculated as 6 +(11 ×4) +(3 ×5) +(15 ×2). Wizard-first grants INT/WIS saves, not Artificer CON proficiency; War Caster's concentration Advantage does not apply to every CON save or waive costly materials.

Manifold does not replace War Caster, skill Expertise, Alert or Alchemist's non-artisan Herbalism proficiency and subclass features. Native proficiencies still work with ordinary kits when the item is unavailable or unattuned.

Magic Initiate adds a free level-1 Mage Armor cast per Long Rest to the existing spell entry. Normal components/duration and slot costs for further casts apply. The feat supplies the existing Mage Hand/Prestidigitation selections, allowing Mind Sliver/Shocking Grasp to occupy Wizard choices. Additional casting sources do not create duplicate rows, book spaces or slot pools. Book placement does not change casting class or actual class-level access; catalogue inclusion alone does not establish revised class access.

## Spell operation

Use recorded 2024 definitions, except the explicitly retained Tasha's Otherworldly Guise. Shared higher slots allow eligible upcasts, not higher Artificer or Wizard spell access. Preserve normal actions, triggers, components and the 2024 slot-per-turn casting restriction. Ordinary eligible book entries are ready under campaign policy; Wizard Ritual Adept additionally covers eligible unprepared book rituals.

| Spell or interaction | Supporting rule |
|---|---|
| Cure Wounds | Level 1: 2d8 + INT healing, mean 15 for Carol; +2d8 per higher slot. Medicine adds nothing. |
| Healing Word | Bonus Action, 60 ft; level 1: 2d4 + INT, mean 11; +2d4 per higher slot. |
| Ray of Sickness | Action, 60 ft, ranged spell attack; 2d8 Poison and Poisoned until end of Carol's next turn on a hit, without an additional CON save; +1d8 per higher slot. Immunities apply. Necromancy receives no Evoker bonus. |
| True Strike | One weapon attack using casting ability, with the total-level-15 +2d6 Radiant rider; no Extra Attack, doubled INT or Evoker bonus. Its fresh firing-time use follows Aetherlock rules; it is not a stored payload or burst-wide cast. |
| Faerie Fire | Concentration, DEX save in a 20 ft cube; attack Advantage requires seeing the affected target. |
| Identify | One minute, or eleven as a ritual; reusable 100 gp pearl still required. Rituals avoid a slot, not components or concentration. |
| Detect Magic | Selected Artificer entry; normal ritual and concentration rules apply. |
| Message / Mending | Message is private communication at 120 ft without concentration. Mending takes one minute, repairs an eligible one-foot break, and does not restore lost magic. |
| Mind Sliver / Shocking Grasp | Use the sheet's current damage/effects. Shocking Grasp prevents Opportunity Attacks on a hit, without legacy all-Reaction denial or metal-armor Advantage. Mind Sliver's next-save penalty has its normal timing window. |

Empowered Evocation adds INT to one eligible Wizard Evocation damage roll per casting, not every ray or spell school. Disintegrate, Mind Sliver, Artificer spells and ordinary gun damage do not receive it. Mean damage is not guaranteed DPR. Potent Cantrip's reduced damage does not add a missed spell's other effects. Shield adds its normal temporary AC bonus without stacking repeated copies; Mirror Image intercepts attacks rather than raising AC and does not protect against save-only effects. Shield and Counterspell compete for the Reaction.

Mage Armor is a non-concentration unarmored base; robes add no armor bonus. Guise, Faerie Fire, Web, Animate Objects and Wall of Force compete for concentration. Animate Objects requires eligible physical objects, commands, reach, survival and concentration. Fabricate requires raw materials, normal casting time/size limits and appropriate proficiency for skilled craftsmanship; it does not make magic items or grant automatic siege/damage benefits.

Healing Word/Ray of Sickness are automatic Alchemist grants, not extra item-plan choices or free casts. They occupy normal entries with grant provenance. The sheet owns the selected spells.

## Artificer creations and elixirs

The working class-3 transcription allows four known plans and two active creations. Creations are produced after a Long Rest; exceeding capacity replaces the oldest. They do not all vanish at the next Long Rest like elixirs. Permanent acquisitions, gifted property and separately owned items are not silently class replicas. No level-6 conversion/recharge feature applies. The sheet owns selected plans and active creations.

Tinker's Magic's temporary mundane gear is distinct from replicas, elixirs, permanent potions and custom ammunition. Respect Artificer tool-in-hand casting requirements; a component pouch alone does not replace them. No Artificer-5 Alchemical Savant or level-9 restorative benefit applies at class level 3.

After a Long Rest, holding Alchemist's Supplies creates two random experimental elixirs. Roll d6 for each; 6 permits choosing the effect. A Bonus Action drinks/administers one within 5 ft. A Magic action plus one spell slot creates one chosen extra elixir; higher slots do not automatically increase quantity or strength. Unused elixirs vanish at the next Long Rest. No concentration requirement is stated; compatible different effects may coexist, while repeated copies do not automatically stack.

| Effect | Class-3 value |
|---|---|
| Healing | 2d8 + INT HP; mean 15 for Carol |
| Swiftness | Speed +10 ft for one hour |
| Resilience | AC +1 for ten minutes |
| Boldness | +1d4 to attacks/saves for one minute |
| Flight | Fly 10 ft for ten minutes |

Alchemist halves potion crafting time. This is not a universal discount or speed increase for all items or ammunition, and daily elixirs are not permanent crafted potions.

## Tools and crafting

Owning a kit does not grant proficiency. Native proficiency adds proficiency bonus once with appropriate equipment. Manifold requires attunement and a Magic action to transform into one artisan-tool set, granting proficiency while used. It supplies neither simultaneous kits, an entire workshop, Herbalism nor Thieves' Tools. It grants no spell attack/DC bonus or borrowed cantrip. Native and item proficiency do not stack into Expertise or double proficiency bonus.

If a called skill check and proficient tool use both apply, the combined rule can grant Advantage. For example, relevant INT (Investigation) stonework with Manifold in Mason form uses Investigation Expertise with Advantage, without another +5. A plain STR masonry action instead uses its applicable ability modifier and proficiency bonus; Investigation Expertise does not apply to every tool action. A different ability requires DM adjudication, not automatic INT because Carol is an inventor.

The table is a menu of proficient applications using Carol's abilities and proficiency bonus, not extra grants or owned kits. Check bonuses are totals, not additions to spell attacks. Basic crafting normally requires proficiency, materials and time rather than an automatic tool check.

## Item handling and optional equipment

The sheet owns current inventory and attunements. Held/attuned Wand +1 improves applicable spell attacks and ignores Half Cover; it does not raise DC, damage or gun aim. Review hands before applying held-item benefits during a Two-Handed rifle attack. Goggles provide worn Darkvision, not bright daylight. Enduring Spellbooks protect book/writing from fire, water and aging, not every form of destruction, additional capacity or combat benefits.

Alchemy Jug weighs 12 lb full or empty. A Magic action chooses a listed liquid and Utilize uncorks/pours it; only that type is available until next dawn, within its daily limit. Recorded examples: acid 8 oz, basic poison 4 oz, oil 1 quart, fresh water 8 gallons; other listed choices include beer, honey, mayonnaise, vinegar, saltwater and wine. It makes no arbitrary potions, spell components or ammunition.
