# Milestone 5 Plan

Purpose: the working plan for Milestone 5, the gameplay loop, as [Milestones](Milestones.md) defines it: the action inside a campaign. Status: drafted 2026-10-24 at 4:45 pm PDT (America/Los_Angeles), right after the user accepted Milestone 4. The user agreed to this plan and its acceptance list on 2026-10-24 at 6:23 pm PDT (America/Los_Angeles); S0 starts with the snapshot and spellbook discussion. Decisions live in the documents linked below; this plan orders the work and lists what still needs the user's answer.

Selected decisions: Milestone 5 stays **one milestone**, built together rather than in separate steps, with pauses to discuss and clarify along the way, because its parts are linked and building them apart would mean redoing earlier work (user decision, 2026-10-24 at 4:50 pm PDT (America/Los_Angeles)). **Simple NPCs** come into it: NPCs made by the campaign's DMs as ordinary characters, at any level of detail from a name and HP to a full sheet, usable in the turn order; monster stat blocks and imports stay later (user decision, 2026-10-24 at 4:50 pm PDT (America/Los_Angeles)). The roadmap's scope (Milestones): specify, then build, turns, initiative, rests and conditions; then the casting workflow (holds, execution-time approval, resolution, response polls, rolls and effects on the game clock). Carried into this milestone by the user: the Spellround and scroll-snapshot work, the spellbook rethink, spent scrolls and the play addresses ([Notes for Milestone 5](Milestones.md#notes-for-milestone-5)).

Open questions: listed by topic under [Questions for the user](#questions-for-the-user), asked one topic at a time, starting with the scope.

| Rolls | A target's player rolls their own save; the DM may take over; configured, custom-expression or manual-total input; one prompt accepts one result | [Roll ownership](../Systems/Spell%20Effects%20and%20Active%20State.md#roll-ownership-and-input-modes) |
| Partial automation | Automated, unknown and manual parts are marked; manual outcomes go through the same approval and log | [Structured effects](../Systems/Spell%20Effects%20and%20Active%20State.md#structured-effects-and-manual-adjudication) |
| Scrolls | A loose scroll uses its scribe's snapshot; a bound scroll uses the current caster; a Spellround's scroll is consumed on release | [Spell Containers](../Systems/Spell%20Containers%20and%20Consumable%20Sources.md) |
| Authority | DMs approve at execution; a solo owner uses DM mode with no bypass; delegates submit for a character they control; Rules check authority, not game math | [Campaigns and Shared Data](../Systems/Campaigns%20and%20Shared%20Data.md#campaign-membership) |

## Questions for the user

**A. Scope** — answered 2026-10-24 at 4:50 pm PDT (America/Los_Angeles): one milestone, built together with pauses to discuss; simple DM-made NPCs included (see Selected decisions).

**B. Recorded in [Turns, Encounters and Time](../Systems/Turns%2C%20Encounters%20and%20Time.md).

**C. Action budget** — answered 2026-10-24 at 5:09 pm PDT (America/Los_Angeles): actions, bonus actions and Reactions are tracked and over-use is flagged for the DM, who may still approve; Reactions refresh at the start of the character's turn; movement is left out until the new Milestone 6, Maps (real Firebase becomes Milestone 7). Recorded in [Turns, Encounters and Time](../Systems/Turns%2C%20Encounters%20and%20Time.md#action-budget).

**D. Rests and downtime** — partly answered 2026-10-24 at 5:28 pm PDT (America/Los_Angeles): rests restore what the published 2024 rules say. The user widened the rest of the topic into **downtime**, everything between encounters (resting, crafting, scribing and other activities), which happens at any real-life time, often without the DM present; it needs careful design on both the systems and the experience level before building: how much time passes and who knows it, how players plan several dependent activities, how requests stay current while the DM is away, and interruptions. Downtime model decided 2026-10-24 at 5:55 pm PDT (America/Los_Angeles): a downtime scene on the encounter machinery, open until the DM ends it by entering the time that passed; activities one at a time with a per-character summary; split-able jobs except rests; players don't see the length. Recorded in [Downtime](../Systems/Turns%2C%20Encounters%20and%20Time.md#downtime). Decided 2026-10-24 at 6:03 pm PDT (America/Los_Angeles): an open floor without turns, responding by adding activities (even before approval), state shown as if pending activities are approved, "waits for" links between characters' activities, crafting and trading as separate steps, and automatic start when an encounter ends. Decided 2026-10-24 at 6:07 pm PDT (America/Los_Angeles): activity types are modules, each with its own logic, so new ones are added without changing the machinery; Milestone 5 builds the machinery with Short Rest, Long Rest and the note with a duration, crafting and scribing come as modules in 5b and trading in 5c. **Design principle for the whole project** (user, 2026-10-24 at 6:07 pm PDT (America/Los_Angeles)): keep things open and modular, so new kinds (activities, and the like elsewhere) are added as modules that bring their own logic, never hard-coded into the core.

## Acceptance (agreed 2026-10-24 at 6:23 pm PDT (America/Los_Angeles))

Drafted 2026-10-24 at 6:16 pm PDT (America/Los_Angeles) for the confirmed scope. Each item gets a unit, emulator or browser test, as in Milestones 3 and 4.

- **Carried over:** Carol's Spellrounds are one Spellround item whose lines carry their scroll, in the content and in the user's saved data; every scroll keeps a scribed-formula snapshot; opening a scroll or Spellround shows the full spell; core entries live in kind folders and Carol resolves exactly as before.
- **NPCs** (changed 2026-10-24 at 8:59 pm PDT (America/Los_Angeles), user decision): a DM makes an NPC template in the campaign's NPCs folder (from name, HP, AC and initiative up to a full sheet); `core/NPCs` holds complete SRD 5.2.1 Goblin, Bandit and Skeleton with their gear; deploying one or several into an encounter makes numbered instances that each roll initiative automatically and are tracked like characters; an ended encounter stays as read-only history.
- **Going down** (added 2026-10-24 at 8:59 pm PDT (America/Los_Angeles), user decision): in Vanilla+ damage can take hit points below 0; a character at 0 or below is Unconscious and wakes at 1 or more; healing adds to the negative number; resistances and Temporary Hit Points apply by the 2024 rules.
- **Encounters and turns:** the DM starts an encounter with chosen characters and NPCs; players roll or type initiative and the DM can for anyone; ties go to who entered first; the DM reorders or adds someone mid-fight; turns advance, rounds count from 1, and each round adds six seconds to the campaign clock.
- **Action budget:** what a request uses is tracked, and over-use is shown to the player and flagged to the DM, who can still approve.
- **Casting:** Carol casts Mage Armor through review, confirm (her slot moves into the request's hold), Next Turn and DM approval at her turn; her AC becomes 16 and the effect expires on the clock. Cancelling or rejecting another request returns its hold exactly once.
- **Response polls:** the Shield example end to end: an attack would hit, a poll opens, Shield is submitted as Instant, the hit is rechecked against the new AC, and a fresh poll closes before damage.
- **Rolls:** a target's player is prompted for their save; the DM can take over; configured, custom-expression and manual totals all work and are logged.
- **Concentration:** starting a second concentration spell at full capacity asks which to replace; becoming Incapacitated ends concentration.
- **Conditions:** all fifteen 2024 conditions exist as definitions; Hold Person applies Paralyzed and prompts the repeat save; Exhaustion's penalty applies by itself; acting while Incapacitated warns but is not blocked.
- **Downtime:** ending an encounter starts a downtime scene; players add activities while the DM is away (a long rest, a note with a duration), chain on one another with "waits for", and see projected state; the DM sees each character's summary and ends the scene by entering the time that passed; rests restore by the 2024 rules, an unfinished rest gives nothing, and the clock moves.
- **Items in play:** drinking a potion and firing the Aetherlock (a round and its charges) go through the same request, hold, approval and resolution.
- **Table pages:** `/Table` and `/Table/Dungeon_Master` show the participants, turn order, current turn, round, clock and log, live; each view shows what its role needs.
