The reported Monk attack bonus hits AC 22 on 7+, a 70% ordinary hit chance before Advantage/Disadvantage; the proposed AC is not an overwhelming defense against every member. Large differences in reported HP/AC also mean one uniform damage assumption is insufficient for every target.
